## Ownership decisions (patched in the master COPY only; upstream files untouched)
- MG2 owns the cursor and the ki motes from f243. In mg1.html, the motes are off and the cursor is hidden at f>=243.
  MG1's `charge_fx` stays TRUE on purpose: the same flag drives MG1's camera push-in 1.00->1.03 (roll + panels), which MG2's charge layer and shatter start positions are built to match. The patch fails loudly if MG1's code changes.
- MG2 draws the band f261-276. MG3 shows its band from f277 (MG3 v16:12). The name pose matches across MG2/MG3/MG4 (baselines 1707/1993).
- Shake f261-264: MG2 shakes its own layers. MG1 has ended by then (f260), the master bg is a flat colour and MG3 is hidden until f277, so no other layer needs it.

## Renders
- Proxy: `./comp.sh proxy` renders the 2160 layout at 1080 (`--resolution portrait`) and downscales it to 540.
  A 0.25 CSS-scale wrapper does NOT work: the roll nested inside MG1 disappears.
- 4K: `./comp.sh 4k` refuses to start if another heavy job is running. HyperFrames delivery crf 8 intermediate -> x264 crf 12 -tune film yuv420p + AAC 320k -cutoff 20000, +faststart. The intermediate is deleted.
  Capture took 7m12s and the whole job about 11 min (21:49-22:00 IST).
- Verified: 450 frames, 18.000 s, 2160x3840, 25 fps, yuv420p H.264 High. Audio is AAC 48 kHz stereo. -14.1 LUFS, -4.6 dBTP true peak, no black frames, moov first.
