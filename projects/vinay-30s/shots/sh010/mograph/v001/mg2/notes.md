# MG2 SPECTRUM STRIKE (f336-f386), v001

- HyperFrames project. `index.html` is the 4K (2160x3840) root and `proxy/index.html` is the 540x960 root. Both mount `compositions/mg2.html` (scene id `mg2`) at data-start 0. The root clock is in master seconds and the duration is 15.48 s (f0-f386). Only f336-f386 has pixels; everything else is transparent.
- Generated by `python3 build.py`, which combines `src/mg2.template.html`, cues.json (HIT, SILENCE, C6/H0 frames, act3_window, palette) and `../name_layout.json`, and inlines `../kit/kit.js`. Edit the template, never compositions/mg2.html.
- `python3 make_layout.py` writes `../name_layout.json` from the real Unbounded-900 metrics. Settings: cap 230 px (size 306.667), tracking +0.02em, GPOS kerning A-Y and R-O, centred on the ink box. The widest line is KRISHAN at 1829.6 px, under the 1880 limit, so no scale is applied. Verified on a 4K snapshot: the rendered White Hot pixels land in the published boxes to within 1 px.
- H0 plate: `assets/H0.mp4` is a symlink to `color/v001/plates/H0.mp4` when it exists, otherwise to `edit/v001/plates/H0.mp4`. For v001 that is the UNGRADED edit plate. To pick up the graded plate, re-run build.py.
- The scene draws the following: charge spirals (option `drawCharge`, on by default), the kit light point f357-364, the f365 flash (80% / 35% / 0), the burst (FX.glow), 8 hue rays, the shockwave, 220 kit sparks (FX.sparks; all dead by f386), and the name. The name forms from 8 additive hue copies (8-sample motion blur) plus the White Hot master at scale 1.35 to 0.96 to 1.00 (f374). A spectral rim from f372 cools to a Champion Gold glow and Gold Deep keyline, complete at f386. The window slams open f364-f367 with H0 inside. Shake is applied to the footage and rays only.
- Not done inside the sub-comp (comp lead): the 1.00 to 1.04 whole-frame push on f336-356 is applied only to MG2's own layers; Ink base under f357-364; the 18 px shockwave refraction of lower layers is approximated by soft light bands.
- Proxy: `mg2_proxy_540.webm` (VP9 with alpha, 387 frames, frame n = master frame n). Review: `mg2_review_540.mp4` (f336-386 over Ink with the bed, 51 frames). The proxy render took about 2 min.
